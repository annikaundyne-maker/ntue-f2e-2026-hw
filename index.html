vegetable

Carrots are crunchy, sweet root vegetables that bring vibrant color to every meal. Packed with beta-carotene, dietary fiber, and essential vitamins, they are perfect for raw snacking, fresh salads, or hearty homemade soups.

Corn is a versatile and nutritious grain that can be enjoyed in various forms. Whether grilled, boiled, or added to salads and soups, it provides a delightful sweetness and a good source of fiber and vitamins.

Peppers are crisp, colorful vegetables that add a delightful crunch and mild heat to any dish. Packed with vitamin C and antioxidants, they are perfect for roasting, stuffing, or adding a fresh kick to your favorite recipes.
